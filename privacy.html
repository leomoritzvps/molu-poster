molu poster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

“molu poster” is a private, non-commercial desktop tool operated by Viktoria Park (“we”). It is used only by its operator to upload the operator’s own DJ video clips to the operator’s own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign up.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator’s own YouTube channel and to read basic channel information (channel name, ID, subscriber and video counts). By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page.

What data the tool processes

  • Account authorization tokens issued by TikTok, Meta (Instagram) and Google (YouTube) after the account owner logs in and grants access.
  • Basic account information returned by these platforms (e.g. account name, ID, follower and video counts) to confirm which account is connected.
  • Videos and captions that the operator chooses to publish.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the operator’s own videos to the operator’s own accounts and to check whether a post was published successfully. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing of data

No user data, including YouTube API data, is shared with any third party. It is only sent to the platform it belongs to (e.g. a video to YouTube) via that platform’s official API.

Cookies and device data

The tool runs locally on the operator’s computer. It does not store, access or collect cookies or other information on users’ devices, apart from the access tokens kept in the macOS Keychain.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only locally on the operator’s computer in the encrypted macOS Keychain. No data is stored on a server operated by us, and no data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are transmitted directly to the respective platform via its official API.

Third-party platforms

Content published through the tool is subject to the terms and privacy policies of TikTok, Meta/Instagram and Google/YouTube. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the security/app settings of TikTok, Instagram/Facebook and Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access or deleting the tool removes all stored tokens; no copies are kept.
